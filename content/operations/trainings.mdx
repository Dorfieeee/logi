# Trainings

Trainings are practice-focused scheduled sessions. The page keeps them separate from competitive matches so communities can plan both without mixing their purpose or completion flow.

**Trainings** uses the same list as [Matches](/wiki/operations/matches#the-match-list):
upcoming trainings grouped by week with their sign-ups and the sign-up
deadline, recently finished trainings below them, and **Played** with how many
passed and failed. Search by name and filter by game. A **Drafts** tab appears
for managers only while a training draft exists.

<Steps>

### Create a training (manager)

Open **Trainings** and choose **New training**. It opens the same five-step flow as a [new match](/wiki/operations/matches#create-a-match-manager) with a training template: no opponent and no map. The name comes from the template until you change it, and work in progress is saved as a draft that only managers see.

### Set an accurate start and end

In the **Time** step, set the date and meeting time. The template gives registration close and duration; **Change for this training only** adjusts them. An end time helps members understand the commitment and helps managers plan subsequent activity. A template can also post the announcement a number of hours before the start, so the training shows in calendars before Logi posts its Discord registration announcement.

### Share the plan

Add the relevant details and use the server’s normal communication process so people know what the training is for.

### Change it before it happens

The training page shows the training in short lines (training, time, sign-ups,
Discord) beside its Discord announcement as members see it now. Managers open
**Edit training** there, or **Edit** on a line, to change it in the same five
steps, filled in with the saved training; see
[edit a match](/wiki/operations/matches#edit-a-match-manager). The **Sign-ups**
step of a training holds the **roles needed to sign up** and the **roles for
passing**, which Logi gives to everyone marked as passed. **More options** in
the first step holds the description, notes and thumbnail. **Save changes**
updates the announcement; a concluded training remains editable for corrections.

### Complete it afterwards

When the training has taken place, use the completion action if your role permits it. Logi requires a completion result for every attendee before it will conclude the training. This keeps the list and history accurate.

Each evaluated participant gets a training result DM in the clan language:
**Passed** with the roles for passing they received, or **Not passed** with a
pointer to the clan calendar for the next date.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
    Do not mark a training complete just because its scheduled time passed. Only
    complete it once the community’s operational record is ready.
</Callout>

## Training rules that differ from matches

Trainings use a simple **Attend** or **Not attending** response; they do not
offer groups, general signup, match membership-status restrictions, or forum
posts. A training can remain open for sign-up after its status becomes
**starting**, right up to its registration deadline. Its event status still
concludes automatically at game end during status reconciliation, so record
completion results before then when attendance history matters.
