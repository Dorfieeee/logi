# Teams

Logi keeps one **team catalogue** for every workspace. Each entry is one
sporting team in one game, with a name, an optional short code, logo,
description and up to three links. Hell Let Loose and Wardogs have separate
catalogues: a team called the same in both games is two separate entries.
Teams do not need a Logi account or a Discord community.

Logi's global administrators maintain the catalogue. Workspaces do not create
or edit teams themselves: they browse the catalogue, select teams in their
matches, and **request** a missing team or **suggest a change** to an existing
one. A Logi administrator reviews each request.

Open **Configuration → Teams** in the workspace navigation. The page is
available to workspace administrators only.

## Choose the game

The page follows the game selector in the sidebar:

- **All games** shows one section for Hell Let Loose and one for Wardogs. The
  two catalogues are never mixed.
- **Hell Let Loose** or **Wardogs** shows only that game's catalogue.
- **Hell Let Loose: Vietnam** has no team catalogue; the page says so.

A section for a game that is not enabled in the workspace's clan settings still
lists the catalogue and accepts requests, with a note and a link to the
settings: its teams can be used in matches only after the game is enabled.

## Browse the catalogue

Each team shows its logo (or, without one, its short code or the initials of
the first two words of its name), name, short code, description and links.
Links open in a new tab. Archived and merged teams are not listed.

Search matches names and short codes. Lists load 50 teams at a time; use
**Load more** for the next page. A search shows the 50 best matches.

Catalogue reads, team requests and snapshot refreshes are limited to 120
requests per minute for each administrator in a workspace. Above that, Logi
asks you to wait a moment and try again.

## Request a new team

<Steps>

### Open the request

Select **Request a new team** in the game's section, or **Request a new team**
in a match's team picker (see [Matches → Teams](/wiki/operations/matches#teams)).

### Enter the team's details

The name is required and may have up to 120 characters. The optional short code
(up to 16 characters) is shown where space is tight. The optional description
may have up to 500 characters, and you can add up to three `https` links, such
as the team's website or Discord invite. Leading, trailing and repeated spaces
are removed.

### Upload a logo (optional)

Choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP image of at most 2 MiB and at most 4096×4096
pixels. Animated images are rejected. Logi stores a normalized copy no larger
than 512×512 and shows it as a preview. **Remove logo** detaches it. Each
administrator can upload 10 images every 10 minutes in a workspace, counting
team logos and public panel banners together; above that, the form says how
many seconds to wait before uploading again.

### Add a note and send

The optional **Note for the administrators** (up to 500 characters) can explain
anything that helps the review. Select **Send request**. If the connection
fails, select **Send request** again in the same form: the retry is recognised
and does not create a second request. Your inputs stay in the form after any
error.

</Steps>

If a team with the same name already exists in that game's catalogue (ignoring
letter case and repeated spaces; accents still count), the form says so instead
of sending the request: select that team, or suggest a change to it.

## Suggest a change

Select **Suggest a change** next to a team. The form starts with the team's
current details; change what should be different and add a note. A change
request must change at least one detail; the note alone is not a change. The
current logo stays proposed unless you upload a new one or remove it.

## Your team requests

**Your team requests**, below the catalogue, lists the requests sent from this
workspace, newest first. Each shows the team, whether it is a new team or a
change, the game, when it was requested and decided, your note, and its status:

| Status        | Meaning                                                                        |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Pending**   | Waiting for a Logi administrator.                                              |
| **Approved**  | The team was added, or the change applied. **Resulting team** names the team.  |
| **Merged**    | The team already existed; the request was merged into it (**Resulting team**). |
| **Rejected**  | Not accepted. **Reason** explains why.                                         |
| **Cancelled** | Withdrawn by the workspace.                                                    |

A Logi administrator may edit the proposed details before approving them, so
the resulting team can differ slightly from your request. A workspace can have
at most 20 pending requests; **Cancel request** withdraws a pending one.

<Callout type="info">
    Every approval, merge or rejection sends the person who submitted the
    request a Discord direct message, in the workspace's language, with the
    outcome and the reason or the resulting team. If their direct messages are
    closed, the decision still appears in **Your team requests**.
</Callout>

The catalogue has no separate page per team. **Open team in Logi** in an
approval or merge message opens this page for the team's game with the team's
name already in the search; **Open Teams in Logi** in a rejection opens the
game's list.

## Archived and merged teams

Logi administrators can archive a team or merge a duplicate into the team that
stays. Archived and merged teams leave the catalogue and cannot be newly
selected, but nothing is deleted: matches that already used the team keep the
name and logo they saved. Refreshing the snapshot of a merged team on a match
uses the team it was merged into.

<Callout type="info">
    A connected website can read the active catalogue only when its key has the
    separate **Team directory** (`teams`) grant. That grant never allows the
    website to change the catalogue or to send team requests.
</Callout>

## Use teams in a match

The match editor has a **Teams** section for Hell Let Loose and Wardogs
matches. See [Matches → Teams](/wiki/operations/matches#teams) for slots, sides,
snapshots and when the selection is frozen.

## Import teams from league directories (global administrators)

Open **Administration → Team catalogue** (`/en/dashboard/teams`) and choose
the game tab. The **Wardogs** tab offers **Scrape teams from Wardogs League**, using
[the full Wardogs League teams page](https://wardogsleague.net/teams?all=1).
The **Hell Let Loose** tab offers **Sync teams from HeLO (2024)**, using the
public data behind [HeLO's 2024 team directory](https://helo-system.de/teams?series=2024).

The button adds missing teams to the **global catalogue for that game**, with
their name, short code (HeLO calls this the tag), and a stored copy of the logo.
It does not register those teams in the competition automatically; use the
competition's team picker afterwards.

A team is skipped if its name **or** short code already exists in that game's
catalogue, ignoring letter case and extra spaces. Archived and merged entries
also count as existing. Existing records, names, codes and logos are preserved.
Repeated imports are safe, including imports started at the same time.

The result shows created, skipped and failed counts, and the names of failed
teams. If a logo cannot be downloaded or normalized, that team is not created;
retry the import after the provider recovers. Teams with no source logo are
created without one. Imported GIF logos use a static first frame. Source
changes can require an importer update; a failed source read creates no teams.
This import is available only to Logi global administrators.

## Register teams in a competition (global administrators)

Open **Administration → Competitions → Manage → Teams**. Choose a division,
open **Choose teams**, and tick as many catalogue teams as you need. Search
matches names and short codes; selected teams stay selected while you search.
**Select all matching** selects the matching unregistered teams across all
catalogue pages, up to the competition's remaining capacity (500 teams total).
Use **Clear selection** or remove an individual selected chip to adjust the
selection, then select **Done** and **Register … teams** once.

The complete batch is validated before any registrations are added. An archived,
merged, missing or other-game team rejects the batch; your selection remains
available to correct and retry. Teams already registered by another
administrator or by a retried submission are skipped without moving them to a
different division. The same catalogue team may be registered in several
competitions; each competition keeps its own fixtures and results.

## Participating in competitions

Clan administrators enroll their linked teams from **Competitions** in the clan
sidebar. Registration can be closed, automatic or subject to Logi administrator
approval. A competition can require a minimum number of clan members who have
signed into Logi; members do not need verified Steam accounts or to use the
other clan tools. Withdrawal preserves the team's competition history. See
[match and competition guidance](/wiki/public/matches-and-competitions).

## Competitions-only workspace

Administrators opening a new, unconfigured clan are asked whether to use the
full Logi toolset or **Competitions only**. The latter keeps the sidebar focused
on the dashboard, competitions, teams, players and settings. It changes
navigation rather than permissions, stops no integrations and deletes no data.
You can change the clan-wide preference at any time in **Clan settings →
Workspace mode**, including when an existing clan wants the simpler menu.
