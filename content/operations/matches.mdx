# Matches

A match is a competitive event with additional match details. Members can use the list to find upcoming or previous matches. Managers can create, edit, prepare and conclude matches; Logi's global administrators link matches to competition fixtures.

## The match list

**Matches** lists matches and trainings together, soonest first, grouped by
week in the clan's time zone (**This week**, **Next week**, then **Week of …**).
Each row shows the date and start time, the name and category, the game, map
and side (and the competition, when the match is linked to a fixture of a
published competition), how many players signed up and the event's phase:

- **Sign-ups until …** while registration is open (the weekday within the
  next six days, otherwise the date);
- **Roster missing** or **Roster · draft** once registration has closed and the
  roster is not published yet;
- **Roster published**, or how many rostered players have not confirmed;
- after play, the score with **Result awaiting confirmation**, **Victory ·
  confirmed** (or defeat, draw, corrected), **No result yet**, or for a
  training how many passed and failed.

**Upcoming** shows what is still ahead with up to three events played in the
last two weeks below it; **Played** shows past events, newest first, thirty at
a time with **Show more**. Filter by **Matches** and **Trainings**, by game, or
search by name, opponent or map; **Clear filters** brings everything back.

### Drafts (manager)

A match saved with **Save as draft** in the new-match flow, or autosaved while
you fill it in, is a draft: the bot does not announce it and members never see
it. Drafts appear only in the
**Drafts** tab (with their number, for example **Drafts · 1**), never under
Upcoming or Played. Each shows **not announced**, the **Draft** label and, when
no opponent is chosen yet, **no opponent yet**. Open a draft to continue in the
new-match form and publish it from there.

### Waiting for you (manager)

At the top, **Waiting for you** lists up to three tasks of each kind:

- **Publish the roster** for matches whose sign-ups have closed, soonest
  first, with the start and the open slots;
- **Confirm the result** for played matches whose imported, collected or
  entered result has not been confirmed yet, with its source and score;
- **Check attendance** for matches played in the last two weeks whose roster
  was published but whose attendance has not been closed yet, with the number
  of rostered players who did not confirm.

Each task opens the matching tab of the match. The result state is read fresh
on every visit, so a confirmed result leaves the list straight away. A manager
also lands on the roster or result tab when opening a row that needs one.

## Recurring matches

**Recurring** lists match series in the same rows as the match list; each row
starts with its schedule, for example **repeats every Wednesday**. A match
created with **Repeat every week** is a weekly series: Logi creates the next
matches of the series on its own, two weeks ahead, at the same local time in
the clan's time zone, with the same teams, map, sign-up and Discord settings
and empty sign-ups. Each one is announced like any other match. The bot creates
them, so they appear only while the Logi bot is running. To change or stop a
series, choose **Edit series**: it opens the match that carries the series in
the [edit flow](#edit-a-match-manager) at the **Time** step. Changes there
apply to the dates Logi creates from then on; switch off **Repeat every week**
and save to stop the series. Matches already created stay as they are, and
editing one of them changes only that date. Monthly repeat settings are only
labels for now: Logi does not create monthly matches.

## Create a match (manager)

Select **Matches** in the active workspace and choose **New match** (or **New
training** under Trainings). Creating takes five steps; the step pills at the
top show where you are, and you can jump to any step. Beside the steps,
**Preview in Discord** shows exactly the announcement card the bot posts, in the
clan's bot language: the ping above it, the team codes and category, the sides,
the start, map and meeting, **Sign-ups open** with the counts, the notes and the
five buttons. Discord shows the times in each reader's own time zone; the
message never shows the server password.

<Steps>

### Match

Pick the **game** and a **template** (see
[match templates](/wiki/configuration/presets-and-templates#match-templates)).
The template fills the times, sign-ups and Discord settings of the next steps.
A training template turns the flow into a training: no opponent and no map.
Under **Teams**, your clan is listed with its side; choose the **opponent**
from the global team catalogue and its side. A team missing from the catalogue
can be requested with **Request it**; it becomes selectable once Logi's
administrators approve it. Choose the **map**, **time of day** and, for Hell
Let Loose, the **middle point**. The **name** is filled in from the teams and
the template ("VLK vs ROG · Friendly") until you change it.

### Time

Enter the start date and time. The box below shows the timeline the template
gives: announcement, registration close, meeting and end. **Change for this
match only** lets you adjust those offsets without touching the template.
Switch on **Repeat every week** for a weekly series.

### Sign-ups

Choose which member statuses may sign up. The offered groups and their caps
start from the template and can be changed immediately, while creating the match:
check each group to offer it, adjust its maximum, or enable **General sign-up**.
Optional required Discord roles restrict eligibility; they do not assign roles.
These choices apply to this match and do not change the template or the clan's
group catalogue. In Discord players press **Sign up** and choose a group
in a private picker that shows each group's count; when a capped group is full,
a player who picks it is signed up as a reserve without a group instead, the
bot tells them so ("Tanks are full (6/6)") and the announcement names the full
groups.
The sign-up reminder row shows who gets the daily reminder.

### Discord

Choose the announcement channel and the roster channel (the clan defaults are
preselected), whom the announcement pings, whether the match forum and the
squad voice channels are created, and the server name and password. Only
players on the published roster ever see the password.

### Review

Check the summary; **Edit** goes back to a step. **Publish** creates the match:
the bot posts the announcement (straight away, or when the template's
announcement time is reached) and creates the forum. You build the roster after
registration closes; it starts from the template's squad preset.

### Record the outcome

After play, enter the result and conclude the event when your workflow is complete.

</Steps>

### Drafts

Work in progress is saved as a **draft** a moment after each change ("Draft
saved at …" next to the title), and **Save as draft** in the review step saves
it on purpose. A draft is visible only to the clan's managers in the
dashboard: the bot does not announce it, nobody can sign up, and it is left out
of calendars, public pages, the website feed and `/api/v1`. Open a draft from
the match list to continue where you stopped (the address keeps
`?draftId=…`). **Publish** turns it into the match; **Delete draft** removes it
after a confirmation. Only drafts can be deleted this way.

## Delete a match (manager)

Open the match's **⋯** menu and choose **Delete match**, then confirm the match
name and consequences. This is available for upcoming and concluded matches.
It permanently removes the match, its sign-ups, roster, reminders and event-specific
result records. Shared strategy maps and competition fixtures remain, with the
match association removed. Other dates already generated in a recurring series
remain; deleting the series source stops future generation. Previously collected
player statistics and game-server history are not rolled back.

The bot receives a durable cleanup request for this match's Discord messages,
threads, squad voice channels, scheduled event and participant roles. Shared
announcement, roster and forum channels are kept. Cleanup runs when the bot is
online; temporary Discord failures are retried. Deletion itself succeeds before
Discord cleanup finishes. The dashboard checks the current manager session and
workspace again when deleting; a read-only member cannot delete a match.

Connected websites use the actor-backed `delete` operation of
`POST /api/v1/clan/event-commands` with the current revision and idempotency key.

## Edit a match (manager)

Choose **Edit match** in the **⋯** menu of a match, or **Edit** on a line of its
**Overview**, which opens that step. Editing uses the same five steps and the
Discord preview as creating, filled in with the match as it is saved. The page
is titled **Edit match** under **Matches › match name › Edit**. Every step is
open from the start; there is no autosave and no draft, and the status next to
the title counts your unsaved changes.

- **Match**: the teams and sides, map, time of day, mode (when the map has more
  than one) and middle point, the name and the **category** (instead of a
  template). The game cannot change. A Wardogs match can also take a third team
  (**Add another team**). **Refresh snapshot** beside a saved team takes its
  current name and logo from the team catalogue and saves that at once. **More
  options** holds the description, notes, thumbnail, picture and tactical maps.
  A map Logi cannot read is kept until you choose another one.
- **Time**: the start and the timeline, open for editing. A weekly series is
  described [above](#recurring-matches).
- **Sign-ups**: who can sign up, the **roles needed to sign up**, which groups are offered and their caps,
  sign-up without a group, who gets the sign-up reminder, the **attendance
  reminders** (24, 18, 12 or 6 hours before the meeting) and the **squad
  preset**. Lowering a cap signs nobody out: players who already hold a place
  keep it, and only new sign-ups go to the reserve. New reminder times replace
  the reminders that have not been sent yet. The squad preset is the starting
  point of a roster created later; an existing roster keeps its squads.
- **Discord**: the announcement and roster channels are shown but stay as they
  were when the match was published (the bot manages its messages there). You
  can change the ping, the forum and its **forum topics**, the squad voice
  channels and their category, **attendee and reserve roles** (switching them
  off makes the bot delete the roles it created), the **meeting voice channel**
  Logi reads attendance from, and the server and password.
- **Review**: the four summary lines and **What changes**, every changed field
  as old → new (a new password reads **new password**, never its value).

**Save changes** saves and returns to the match; the bot updates the
announcement and the other Discord messages as after any edit, and players who
signed up stay signed up. **Cancel** returns to the match and asks first when
there are unsaved changes, as does leaving the page another way. A concluded
match can no longer be edited, and opening a draft this way continues it in the
new-match flow.

## The match page

Opening a match shows its name, game, map and time (meeting and start, or when
it was played), **View in Discord** once the bot has posted the sign-up
announcement (until the match is played) and, for managers, a **⋯** menu with
**Edit match** (until the match is concluded), **Open the roster page** and
**Sign-up history**. On a phone
the top shrinks to a back arrow, the match name and the current step ("Step 3
of 5 · Roster"), the Discord tab is left out and the ⋯ menu also holds the
Discord link. Below is the match's progress in five steps:

| Step     | Shows                                                                        |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Draft    | When the match was created, or when its sign-ups open                        |
| Sign-ups | Until when people can sign up, when sign-ups closed, then how many signed up |
| Roster   | Not created yet, a draft to publish before the meeting, or the player count  |
| Match    | The meeting time, then how many players attended                             |
| Result   | Import from the server, awaiting confirmation, confirmed or corrected        |

The current step is highlighted; earlier steps are ticked. A match counts as
closed 15 minutes after its game end even before Logi records it, and a match
with a confirmed or corrected result has every step done. Members see the
result step without the review state, which only managers can read.

The page has five tabs. Before the match the sign-ups tab is called
**Sign-ups** with the number of players signed up; after it, **Sign-ups and
attendance**.

- **Overview**: the match in four lines, as in the review step of the
  new-match flow (match, time, sign-ups, Discord), beside the sign-up
  announcement as players see it now, with the current sign-up counts.
  Managers get **Edit** on each line, which opens that step of the
  [edit flow](#edit-a-match-manager), and **Resync topic thread** when the
  match has a forum with a topic preset. Members see the same lines without
  the links. A date of a weekly series says so; managers can open the series
  from there.
- **Sign-ups and attendance**: see below.
- **Roster**: the [roster board](/wiki/operations/rosters), or a prompt to
  create the roster. Members see only a published roster.
- **Discord**: which messages the bot posted for the match (sign-up
  announcement, match info, match thread, roster update and the Discord event
  with its state), each with a link into Discord, and the channels in use.
- **Result**: the result review (managers) and a link to the imported match
  statistics.

## Sign-ups and attendance (manager)

The **Sign-ups and attendance** tab counts roster players who attended, were
excused or did not attend, reserves who attended, people who declined and
members who never answered. The table lists the roster players in squad order,
then the reserves, with their place, what they said before the match, their
attendance and the points it gives under the workspace's
[membership score rules](/wiki/configuration/members). Filters switch to the
people who declined or did not answer; **Sign-up history** opens the event's
sign-up activity.

- **Attended** records manager-confirmed attendance on the roster, the same
  status as **Confirmed** on the roster board.
- **Before the match** shows **Confirmed attendance**, **Not confirmed**,
  **Reserve**, or **Running late** with the text a player sent through the
  **Running late** button in Discord.
- **Excused** can be set by a manager for any roster player or reserve; it
  scores like a running-late notice (the **Excused absence** rule). A player
  who sent a running-late notice stays at least excused, so **Did not attend**
  is not available for them. Without a notice or an excuse, a player who did
  not come is **Did not attend**.
- Changes show as **Unsaved changes: N** until you **Save attendance**.
- **Load from voice channel** marks roster players and reserves who are in the
  meeting voice channel as attended (see
  [rosters](/wiki/operations/rosters#attendance-and-late-changes)). Next to the
  button Logi says when it was last read, for example "Last read at 20:05: 19
  players in Sraz", and the table marks players it found with **from the voice
  channel**.
- The **No response** filter lists members who never answered the sign-up.
  While sign-ups are open, **Remind** sends them the sign-up reminder by
  Discord DM, at most once an hour per match. The same reminder can be sent from
  Discord with **Remind non-responders** under **Show sign-ups** on the
  announcement; both share the one-hour limit.

When a reminder does not reach everyone, the match **Overview** tells
managers for a week: "The reminder reached 9 of 12 players" with the names of
the players whose Discord direct messages from server members are off,
**Copy names** and a link to Discord's help on allowing server messages. This
covers the reminders managers send and the scheduled ones: the daily sign-up
reminder and the attendance reminders before the meeting. The notice is about
the newest reminder that went out. The line under it says which reminder it
was, when it was sent and who sent it, or "automatically, as scheduled" for a
scheduled one. A player the bot reaches on a later try of the same reminder is
no longer listed. If the bot could not send a manual reminder at all, the
notice says so instead. These failures are not posted to the Discord errors
channel.

Attendance can change until the match is closed. **Close match and award
points** first shows how many players each rule
covers and the points it gives, for example "19 attended from the roster +3",
and how many players' points change. Save attendance first: closing is not
available with unsaved changes. Closing cannot be undone. A match nobody closes
closes itself 15 minutes after its end, with the same points.

## Teams

Hell Let Loose and Wardogs matches have **Teams** in the **Match** step of the
new-match and edit flows. They use the Logi
[team catalogue](/wiki/operations/teams), which Logi's global administrators
maintain for every workspace. Hell Let Loose: Vietnam matches and trainings
have no teams.

- **Your team** (Team A) is the clan's team linked in the catalogue, or the
  team the match saved in that slot, with its side.
- **The opponent** (Team B) is chosen with **Choose opponent**, a searchable
  list of the game's active catalogue teams with logo, name and short code.
  The cross removes it again.
- **Wardogs** matches can take a third team (Team C) when you edit them, with
  **Add another team**.

Sides are optional: **Allies** or **Axis** for Hell Let Loose, **Valkyra**,
**Manticore** or **Lonestar** for Wardogs; **No side** leaves a side open.
Picking a side for one of two teams gives the other team the opposite side.

When the team you need is missing, **Request it** under the teams opens the
[team request form](/wiki/operations/teams#request-a-new-team); the rest of the
unsaved match is kept. A requested team is not selected automatically: once a
Logi administrator approves it (the requester gets a Discord direct message),
it can be chosen like any other team. Until then, the match can be saved
without an opponent.

A team can occupy only one slot, and a side can be given to only one team;
saving a conflict is refused with a message. Choosing teams does not change the
match's roster, attendance or results.

### Saved team details

Saving a newly selected team stores a snapshot of its name, short code and
logo on the match. Later catalogue renames, logo changes, archiving and merging
do not change saved matches. Moving a team to another slot or changing its side keeps
its snapshot; replacing it with a different team captures that team's details.
If a newly selected team is archived before you save, the save is rejected with
a message so you can choose another team.

A saved team that has since been archived stays on the match until you replace
it; archived teams cannot be newly selected. To pick up catalogue changes for a
saved team, use **Refresh snapshot** beside it while editing the match. It works
only while the team is active in the catalogue and the match is not concluded,
and it updates the stored snapshot immediately without saving other changes in
the flow.

When Logi administrators merge a duplicate into another team, **Refresh
snapshot** on the merged team switches that slot to the surviving team and
captures its details, keeping the slot and side.

Once a match is concluded, its teams and snapshots are frozen: the Teams
section becomes read-only and saving other match fields leaves them unchanged.
A match counts as concluded from 15 minutes after its game end, even before
Logi records the conclusion.
Result corrections continue through the reviewed-result workflow below.

## Competition links

Competitions are run by Logi's global administrators, who link a match to a
competition fixture from the competition's management page. The match must be
for the competition's game and not already linked to another fixture. When
teams are assigned to the match, both fixture teams must be among them, so
assign the correct catalogue teams. Workspace managers cannot link matches
themselves. Linking a match does not replace the organiser’s official
competition rules or results process.

<Callout type="info">
    If your match should count for a competition, assign both teams and ask a
    Logi global administrator to link it. Results imported for a linked match
    update the fixture's score.
</Callout>

## Teams on Discord match cards

When teams from the team catalogue are assigned to a match, the announcement
is titled with their short codes ("VLK vs ROG") and its sides row shows each
team's code as a chip with its side and faction emblem in slot order (for
example `VLK` Allies ★ vs `ROG` Axis ✚). The separate team logo cards are gone.
Codes come from the snapshot saved when the team was assigned; later catalogue
edits change them only if that snapshot is refreshed before the match concludes.
Matches without assigned teams keep their name as the title and show the
clan's side.

## The match in Discord

The match keeps one announcement card in the announcement channel while it is
being organised; the bot edits it at every change, and the
[events guide](/wiki/operations/events#what-the-discord-messages-show) lists
each state with its buttons. In short: **Sign-ups open**, then **Sign-ups
closed** while command builds the roster, **Roster published**, **Starting**
from the meeting with the confirmation counts, **In progress** from the start,
and finally **Played** when it is concluded. When a match is concluded, Logi
removes the registration/event-information cards instead of posting a duplicate
result there; the configured **Results** panel is the only Discord result
publication. A match ended before its meeting shows
**Cancelled** and cancels its Discord event.

The card itself shows every offered group, its sign-up count and alphabetically
ordered signed-up names, followed by the people who declined. Use
**Check registration** to review or change your own response. The manager
attendance page still provides the detailed reserve, absence-reason and
non-response tools.

## Result, conclusion, and scoring are separate

Recording a match result stores the result and can update a linked competition
fixture. Concluding the match changes its lifecycle, turns its announcement into
the **Played** card (or **Cancelled** when it ended before the meeting), removes
its roster card, completes or cancels its linked Discord scheduled event, and
triggers the one-time score calculation. The calculation uses the workspace score
settings, roster position, manager-confirmed attendance, decline status, and
absence notices; it does not infer attendance from merely signing up or
acknowledging a reminder.

After an imported scoreboard is saved, Logi refreshes the workspace performance
trend and the trends of the members who played that match in background jobs.
The saved match and conclusion do not wait for those derived charts, so a
performance chart can update shortly after the match result appears.

When a recorded result includes linked roster-player statistics, opted-in
rostered players with an explicitly linked Discord account are eligible for a
personal match recap by Discord DM. An imported player ID alone is not a Discord
recipient. The recap uses
the workspace's configured clan language and opens the matching localized
public-statistics page. Its comparison uses the same previously recorded player
matches shown on that page.

The recap card shows the score and the player's numbers in the clan's
number format, with **View statistics**, **Turn off recaps** and **Open
settings**. Turning recaps off edits the same DM and applies to every clan the
player belongs to in Logi; **Turn on recaps** reverses it.
Turning recaps off blocks pending delivery while that preference is disabled.
An account link that no longer matches the queued recipient also blocks delivery;
old queued recaps without a recorded recipient are withheld. These are best-effort
notifications: a message already in flight may arrive after opting out, and a
failure between sending and recording success can cause a duplicate.

## League fixtures and local clan events

A match event is always local to its clan: registration closing time, meeting
time, roster, attendance, Discord channels, tactics and publication remain the
clan's own choices. Selecting an opponent or a league fixture is optional, so
an internal friendly can stay entirely local.

A global administrator can create a competition, register the global teams and
schedule a fixture. Hell Let Loose fixtures have two teams. Wardogs fixtures
may have either two or three teams. Each participating clan may link its own
local match event to the same fixture; a global administrator can also add or
repair those links when a clan forgets. Linking only checks the game and known
fixture teams. It never exposes another clan's roster, attendance or channels.

When choosing a collected HLL or Warcon game as a result source, Logi suggests
at most ten candidates nearest the event's planned start and end window. It
never attaches a game automatically: a manager must choose the source and then
stage/confirm the result.

The first confirmed linked-event result becomes the fixture's provisional
standings result. Later linked clans can still confirm their own local result.
If its participant scores differ, Logi keeps the earlier fixture score and
marks a result discrepancy for global administrators to resolve; it never
silently overwrites either clan's local record. Fixture standings use fixture
results. Kills, deaths and available Wardogs cash remain game-wide player and
clan statistics rather than separate per-competition totals.

## Review a result (manager)

Open the match's **Result** tab. The score board puts your clan's side first
(found from the side the match was played on and the teams picked for it), with
the team names and logos, the two scores and **Win**, **Loss** or **Draw**.
Before a source is selected it starts from the match's saved team slots instead
of generic participants. A Wardogs match uses all three saved slots; source
factions are matched to the saved team on that side. A faction without a
matching team never borrows another team’s logo by position. Your faction uses
your clan name when no team snapshot matches; each row shows the team name
above its editable faction label. Team names and logos are immutable snapshots from the catalogue,
so a linked clan's team keeps the information captured for this match.
**Where the result comes from** names the
collected server game the scores came from. Both the selected source and the
picker show the provider’s match end date and time in the workspace timezone.
A missing end time is shown as unknown; a start or collection time is never
substituted for it. Before anything is saved, Logi
suggests the game that started closest to the match start (within three hours)
and fills in its scores; **Other game** picks a different game, an existing
imported result, or manual entry. Selecting a source does not approve it.
**Save without confirming** stores a provisional version; **Confirm result**
saves any unsaved edits as a provisional version and then confirms it. An empty
score is unknown; zero is a known score. The **History** column lists each
saved version with its time and who saved or reviewed it.

The form supports two to sixteen participants, including manual Wardogs factions.
The HLL and Wardogs collectors both supply result-source candidates. The picker
shows the ten games that ended most recently, rather than the most recently
fetched records. For an HLL game played on another team's server, use **Submit
match results** below the result review and paste its public
`https://event.valkyriahll.app/games/[id]` link; Logi imports its public
scoreboard and player statistics. A changed or incomplete
source cannot be confirmed; refresh, select the current source and review again.

Confirming or correcting a Wardogs result also saves the linked Warcon games’
player kills, deaths and signed cash delta and refreshes player/clan trends.
Player IDs match verified Steam links or an unambiguous exact platform ID on an
existing workspace member; names alone never match an account. A platform-ID
stats match does not turn that ID into verified identity. Unmatched players are
retained without an account. Replaying recovery does not add duplicate matches.
Changing a result’s linked games replaces their player-stat contribution.

After confirmation, enter a reason and use **Save correction**. The previous
version remains in the recent revision history with its reviewer and timestamp.
A later import does not silently replace a reviewed result. Another manager's
concurrent save requires you to refresh before saving your version.

Player attribution uses active verified Steam links at review time. Manually
entered IDs and player names do not verify an account. Unlinking affects future
reviews; it does not rewrite past review history. Counts do not establish team
membership, attendance or game ownership.

**After confirmation** lists what confirming does:

- the result appears in the channel of the workspace's Discord results panel,
  when one is set up for the game (otherwise the page says it stays in Logi and
  the clan API);
- the configured Discord results panel publishes it; once the match is
  concluded, its registration and event-information cards are removed so the
  result is not duplicated there;
- players get their personal summary by DM, which today is already sent when
  the statistics are imported;
- for a match linked to a competition fixture, a confirmed or corrected HLL
  result fills the fixture score, the same way an imported result does: each
  fixture team takes the score of the Axis or Allies side it played. Without
  both sides known the fixture is left to the competition administrators.

Reviewed results are separate from the legacy imported scoreboard and event
conclusion described above. A confirmed result does not automatically publish
anything on an external website.


## Wardogs match reports

Confirming a Wardogs result with a collected Warcon game saves a reviewed match
report. The match, public player statistics and Discord result card link to the
same report. It shows the teams and their assigned factions, final scores,
faction kills/deaths and money graphs, and searchable player statistics. K/D
always has two decimal places. Missing money or other provider fields remain
unknown. Actual score timelines are shown when collected; older imports may
not include them. Dates use the provider's match end time.

A played match without a recorded result can suggest a nearby completed server
game. Review its end time, map and scores before confirming it; the suggestion
never publishes automatically. You can choose another source on the Result tab.

Opted-in rostered players with linked statistics receive one queued personal
recap when the original Wardogs result is confirmed. If a Wardogs results panel
is enabled, delivery waits until its result card is successfully posted. The
DM includes kills, deaths, K/D, money and a performance graphic. Durable sent
markers prevent replay after an ordinary bot restart. Correcting the result
does not queue another DM and suppresses any original recap still waiting.
Historical report recovery does not send retrospective DMs. Delivery uses the
same recipient and opt-out checks as HLL; a crash between Discord accepting a
DM and saving its sent marker can still cause a retry, as with HLL.
