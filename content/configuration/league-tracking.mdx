# Wardogs League

Logi follows the public [wardogsleague.net](https://wardogsleague.net) site and shows **the whole League**, not only the clan's matches, in Discord as two messages the bot keeps up to date in one channel:

1. **WD League · tabulka** (table): every team of the season with points, matches and how often it finished 1st, 2nd and 3rd.
2. **WD League · nejbližší zápasy** (next matches): the nearest fixtures of the League with teams, map, host and how far their preparation is, and under them the **recent results** of the last seven days.

The clan's own matches keep their announcement, roster and result in the match channels as before.

## Set up the panel

Create the panel in **Settings → Discord panels** with the type **WD League**, choose the channel and send it. The bot posts the table first and the next matches under it, once, and from then on only edits the two messages. Keep the channel for the bot; people discuss the League elsewhere so the panels stay on top.

In the editor you choose what the panel shows (all on by default):

| Option            | Meaning                                                   |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Table             | The League table. Off removes the first message.          |
| Next matches      | The nearest fixtures. Off leaves only the recent results. |
| Recent results    | Results of the last seven days under the fixtures.        |
| Number of matches | How many nearest fixtures to show, 1–10 (6 by default).   |

A workspace has one WD League panel. The panel refreshes every 60 seconds. **Pause** keeps both messages in Discord with the chip **Pozastaveno · správce zastavil obnovování** and the time of the last data, and stops refreshing until you resume.

Developers: the options are `leaguePanelOptionsSchema` with the defaults `DEFAULT_LEAGUE_PANEL_OPTIONS` (`src/domain/wardogs-league/panels.ts`), stored in the panel's `league` field.

## What the messages show

**Table.** A code block with the columns `#`, team code, points (B), matches (Z), 1st, 2nd, 3rd and the team name, so the columns line up and the points stay visible on a phone. Logi computes the points itself from the places published on each match page, with the rule printed there (1st place 3, 2nd 2, 3rd 1 by default); friendlies count when the League publishes a rule for them. Teams are ordered by points, then by more 1st, 2nd and 3rd places, then by fewer matches; teams equal on all of these share the rank. The season is the calendar year. Your team, set by the watched team codes below, is marked with **›** and shown brighter on desktop. Until the first result of the season exists the message says **Tabulka se zobrazí po prvních výsledcích**.

**Next matches.** For each fixture: number, type, the time in each reader's own time zone and how soon it is; each team with code, name, nationality, member count and the faction it plays; map, zone and lighting, or _Mapa po hlasování_ while the map is open; who hosts; and the preparation as chips: rules picked, the map vote and when it closes, the moderator and the ready check, green when done, orange while running, grey before it starts (_Příprava ještě nezačala_ when nothing has started). A fixture whose map is known shows the map image. When the fixtures do not fit one Discord message, the panel shows fewer and says how many more are on the League site. If the League site stops answering, the panel keeps the last data with the chip **Web ligy neodpovídá** and the time of the last data.

**Recent results.** Places 1–3 of every League match of the last seven days. They come from the League site and are not checked by Logi; the clan's confirmed results are in its results panel.

### Published final results

Logi reads the League's **Final placements** cards when the page says **Confirmed** or **Set by admin** and its matching final progress step is done. It keeps the explicitly published place and team identity, including tied ranks. An admin-set result can be final without all leaders submitting; submission count is not used as the result. If the League publishes only second and third place, those places remain unchanged and no first-place team or no-show is invented. Disputed, unknown or incomplete result layouts stay unavailable.

Until collection stores the first result, the table shows its waiting text and the recent results say **Výsledky se zobrazí po prvních výsledcích ligy**. Afterwards a week without results says **Za posledních 7 dní nejsou žádné výsledky ligy.** Deploying a changed results parser schedules retained finished fixtures without results for a bounded re-read; it does not guarantee complete historical coverage beyond the pages the League index and retained fixture store provide. League results remain separate from reviewed native Logi match results.

## Settings → Wardogs League

This page controls collection and the clan's own tracking:

- **Track Wardogs League and match links** turns the League on. **Turning it off deletes both panel messages** from Discord and stops collection; the panel stays configured and is posted again when you turn the League back on.
- **Watched team codes** (initially `VLK`) decide which team is "ours" in the panels and which League matches are tracked automatically for the clan.
- **Links channel**: people may post `https://wardogsleague.net/matches/...` links here. The bot answers once with the match, its time and two buttons: **Otevřít kartu** (the next-matches message) and **Otevřít na webu ligy**. It answers only when the links channel is not the panel channel, never to bots or webhooks, and never for matches Logi found by itself. Reading people's messages needs the Message Content Intent (see Activation).
- Scan and refresh cadences apply to the clan's tracked matches.

Single match cards are no longer posted; cards from earlier versions are deleted automatically.

### Three ways to track a match

1. **Discovery:** matches of a watched team are tracked automatically.
2. **Manual:** paste a public match URL, preview it and choose **Track match**. This pins the match independently of the team filter.
3. **Discord:** someone posts a valid URL in the links channel. Editing or deleting the message removes only its reference. Logi remembers which message named the match for 14 days after it was posted; editing or deleting the message later changes nothing.

**Ignore** withdraws a match from the website collection; repeated scans do not restore it. **Resume** pins it again. **Pause** keeps its data while stopping refreshes. **Create a native Logi match** opens the match editor, and **Logi match for this fixture** links a tracked fixture to an existing Wardogs match in this workspace (one fixture per match). The link never changes sign-ups, rosters, attendance or results.

## Data and freshness

Logi checks the public fixtures and results lists, and refreshes tracked match pages, no more often than every 15 minutes. The scan and refresh settings default to 15 minutes and cannot be set lower. All League reads share a budget of 20 page loads per minute and respect the site's rate limits, so busy periods can delay updates. A failed read keeps the last valid data. Times in Discord adapt to each reader; the dashboard uses Europe/Prague.

## Activation and limits

- Needs the deployed Convex functions and cron, the dashboard and the running bot.
- The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files (map images) and Read Message History in the panel channel.
- Answering posted links additionally needs **Message Content Intent** in the Discord Developer Portal and `LOGI_LEAGUE_MESSAGE_CONTENT=true` in the bot environment, followed by a restart. Only links posted in the last 24 hours are answered.
- Limits: 20 watched team codes; 500 retained tracked matches per workspace; 600 League fixtures in the shared store; three links per message.

## Connected website

Grant the website key **Tracked Wardogs League fixtures** (`league-fixtures`) for **Wardogs**. Then:

- `GET /api/v1/clan/league-fixtures?game=wardogs&limit=50` lists the clan's tracked fixtures (follow `data.nextCursor`), with the `league-fixtures` changes feed for updates.
- `GET /api/v1/clan/league-fixtures/overview?game=wardogs&limit=6` returns what the two panels show: the table of the season and the nearest fixtures of the whole League with the recent results, with your team marked.

Posting, refreshing, pausing and deleting the Discord panels are actions in the dashboard and are not part of the API. Website keys cannot change watched teams, rooms or tracking.
