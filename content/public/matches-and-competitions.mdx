# Matches and competitions

## Public match pages

Public matches are records with imported match statistics, not a live list of
all planned events. An event becomes eligible only when it is a non-training
match and points to its imported match-stat record. This intentionally excludes
draft plans, ordinary events, trainings, and partially imported results.

Open a public match to see its limited statistical projection: participating
sides, time, map information, and results when recorded. A match result can be
imported before a manager has recorded the community-facing outcome, so read
the result and event status separately.

Open a public match to see the information its community has made available: participating sides, planned or completed time, map information, and results when recorded. A match without a result is not necessarily cancelled—it may simply not have been concluded yet.

## Competitions

Standings are calculated from final and forfeit fixtures that have both scores.
For the ECL-style table, cap score is the first ranking value, then regular
wins, total wins, fewer total matches, and team name. Forfeits count as total
matches/wins where their score says so, but not as regular matches/wins. A
scheduled fixture or a fixture without both scores does not change standings.

The public competitions area lists every published competition. Competitions
that Logi's global administrators are still preparing are unpublished and do
not appear on these pages or in the public API. Every competition belongs to
one game (Hell Let Loose or Wardogs), and only matches for that game can be
linked to it.

Open a competition to see its game, how many teams play in how many
divisions, and the official rules and website when Logi knows them. Below,
pick a division in the tabs (the address keeps the choice, so a link opens the
same division). When the competition has play-off fixtures, a **Play-off** tab
gathers them from every division; league and relegation fixtures stay in their
division.

The **Table** lists each team with its **Played** (total matches), **Won**
(total wins) and **Points** (cap score). Teams are shown with their logo from
Logi's team catalogue, or with their short code or initials when they have no
logo; a withdrawn team is marked as such, and a team that represents a public
Logi clan links to that clan's page.

Beside the table, fixtures are grouped by round when the competition records
rounds ("Round 3 · this weekend", "Round 2 · results"), and by week when it does
not. The next round and the last played round are shown; **More rounds** opens
the others. Relegation and forfeit fixtures are labelled, and **Statistics**
opens the linked Logi match when one exists. Competition rules are owned by the
competition organiser; use the official rules link when one is provided.

<Steps>

### Find a match

Use the Community page, a clan page, or a competition fixture to open a public match.

### Check the state

Read the date, event status, and result separately. Each answers a different question: when it is/was scheduled, where it is in the workflow, and whether a score has been recorded.

### Need to change the match?

Only authorised server managers can edit the underlying event in their workspace dashboard.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
    A public match link does not grant access to private briefings, rosters,
    Discord settings, or management actions.
</Callout>


## Wardogs match reports

Wardogs result cards and player match pages link to a shared public match
report. It shows assigned teams and factions, final scores, faction combat
and money graphs, and a searchable player table. Linked players open their
personal match statistics. K/D always has two decimal places; missing provider
metrics are shown as unknown. The displayed date is the match end time.
Actual score timelines appear when collected, so older games can show the
other graphs without a timeline. Discord previews use Wardogs kills, deaths
and money rather than HLL effectiveness fields.
