## Team catalogue and team requests

Logi keeps one global team catalogue per game (Hell Let Loose and Wardogs). Only
global administrators (superadmins) manage it, from **Team catalogue** and
**Team requests**.

On every global administration page (Competitions, Team catalogue, Team
requests, Bot logs, Platform settings) the sidebar switches to the **Global
administration** navigation: it says that these pages cover all of Logi and
every clan, leads back to the clan you came from, groups **Competitions and
teams** and **Operations**, and shows how many team requests are waiting. Workspaces only read the
catalogue: their administrators pick catalogue teams for matches and send a
request when a team is missing or out of date.

On **Team catalogue**, choose the game tab, show **Active**, **Archived** or
**Merged** teams and search by name or short code. Each row shows the team's
short code, whether it is a clan on Logi and how many competitions it plays; a
**Request** marker means a clan's change request is waiting. Choose a team to
edit it beside the list: **Change logo**, name, short code and **Clan on Logi**,
and under **Description and links** a description (up to 500 characters) and up
to three https links, then **Save**. **Where the team is used** lists its
competitions with division and fixture count; saved clan matches keep the name
and logo they were saved with. When a request is waiting, **Open request** leads
to it in Team requests. **Add team** creates a new team. Each team has a name
(unique per game, ignoring letter case and extra spaces). Linking a team to one
Logi workspace records that the team is that workspace's clan; the link grants
no permissions. Logos uploaded here are normalized to a PNG no larger than
512×512 and belong to the platform, not to a workspace.

- **Archive** first asks for confirmation and lists what changes; it hides a
  team from new selection and from connected websites, and saved matches keep
  their snapshot. **Restore** brings it back unless another
  active team has taken the name meanwhile.
- **Merge** retires a duplicate into the team that stays (an active team of the
  same game). The duplicate is archived with a pointer to the kept team, and its
  competition registrations, fixtures and pending requests move to the kept
  team. Saved match snapshots are not rewritten. A merge cannot be undone. Two
  teams that both take part in one competition (both registered, or a fixture
  between them) are different teams there and cannot be merged.
- Archive and **Merge into another team** are under **Duplicate or end of the
  team** in the team's detail; an archived team offers **Restore** there.
- Every save, archive, restore and merge is checked against the version you
  loaded. If someone else changed the team first, the detail shows the latest
  version and keeps your own edits; review it and try again.

On **Team requests**, **Pending** lists the waiting requests oldest first and
**Decided** the approved, merged, rejected or cancelled ones; both can be
narrowed to one game or one clan. A change request names the fields it
changes; a new-team request warns **A similar team already exists** when an
active team of the game has the same name or short code or shares all words of
the shorter name. Choose a request in the list to see it beside the list: the
requester's Logi name (or Discord ID) and clan, the game, how long ago it was
sent, the requester's note and either the proposed team (new-team requests) or,
for a change request, a table of each field as it is now and as proposed, with
the rows that change highlighted and the competitions the team is used in.

- **Approve** accepts the proposal as sent after a confirmation that lists what
  changes.
- **Edit and approve** opens an editable copy of the proposal. Approving a new-team
  request creates the team; approving a change request applies the fields to
  the current team at the version shown, so a team that changed meanwhile has to
  be reviewed again. The requester's logo becomes a platform logo. If an active
  team of the same game already uses the name, you are offered to merge the
  new-team request into that team instead.
- **Merge into existing team** (new-team requests only) records that the
  requested team already exists: choose an active team of the same game.
- **Reject with reason** requires a reason of up to 500 characters.

Every decision sends the requester one Discord direct message in the requesting
workspace's language and clan colour: **The team is in the catalogue**
(**Approved**), **The team was already in the catalogue** (**Merged**, with the
name the requester asked for) or **The team request was not accepted**
(**Rejected**, with the reason and "Send a corrected request in Logi → Teams").
It shows the team's code and name and a button to the team or the clan's
**Teams** page in Logi. The bot retries a failed message a few times with
increasing delays; when the requester's DMs stay closed the request shows
**Could not be delivered**, and the decision itself stands. Requests a workspace
withdraws are shown as cancelled and send no message.

## Platform settings

**Platform settings** chooses the Logi workspace and the Discord channel where
the bot keeps the platform-wide status message and its **Status changes**
thread (see [Platform status channel](/discord-bot-setup#platform-status-channel-superadmins)). Only workspaces with the Logi bot are offered, and the channel is
picked by name from that workspace's Discord channels (announcement and text
channels). If the saved workspace has lost the bot, the page names it and says
that status updates have stopped until the bot is back or another workspace is
chosen.

The public **Service status** page (`/status`, linked from the status dot in the
website footer) lists the monitored services. When the status monitor does not
answer, the page says so instead of showing an empty status.

## Bot logs

Logs document what the bot attempted or observed; they are not a guarantee that
Discord completed a requested change. When diagnosing an issue, compare the log
entry with the affected Discord channel/role and the saved dashboard setting.
The error channel is optional, so a missing Discord error report does not prove
that the bot had no error.

**Bot logs** provides operational visibility into bot activity, including timestamp, level, source, scope, message, and context. Use it to investigate a specific issue, not as a place to expose private data. Copy only the minimum useful diagnostic information into a support report.

It is a superadmin-only page, separate from normal server-manager access. It
reads the application's local system-log database and can filter by free-text
search, level (INFO/WARN/ERROR), source (Next.js or Discord bot), and known
scope. A blank or quiet log table is not a live bot-health check, and an entry
records an application-side attempt or observation rather than a guarantee that
Discord completed the operation.

## Competitions

Competitions are global: Logi's global administrators (superadmins) run them
from **Global administration → Competitions**, and every competition belongs to one game,
Hell Let Loose or Wardogs. Teams come from the global team catalogue; a
workspace is never a competition team. Each write is checked against the
administrator's current dashboard session, so a revoked session stops working
immediately.

A competition's page shows its game, division and team counts and, once it is
published, a link to its public page. Its sections are tabs: **Details**,
**Divisions**, **Teams** and **Fixtures**; a new competition opens on
Divisions, one without teams on Teams, and a running one on Fixtures.
**Fixtures** shows one division (or all) and one phase (League, Playoff or
Relegation) at a time, grouped by round with the latest round first and the
days each round spans. Each fixture shows whether it is linked to a clan's
match, offers **Link to a match** while it is not, and shows **Awaiting clan
confirmation** while an imported result is not yet confirmed by the clan.

- **Create and publish.** A new competition needs a game, name, season and a
  unique address (slug: 2–64 lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens). It starts
  **unpublished** and stays hidden from the public pages and the public API
  until you switch on **Published**. The game cannot be changed later.
  Changing the slug changes the public address. Competitions created before
  the flag existed stay published.
- **Divisions.** Add, rename and reorder divisions; the public page shows them
  in this order. A division can be deleted only when it has no teams and no
  fixtures.
- **Teams.** Search the catalogue and register active teams of the
  competition's game into a division. Archived, merged and other-game teams
  cannot be registered, and a team can be registered once per competition. You
  can move a team to another division only while it has no league fixtures in
  its current division, mark it withdrawn or reinstate it, and remove it only
  while it has no fixtures. Missing or duplicate teams are fixed in the team
  catalogue (create, request or merge there); merging catalogue teams moves
  their registrations and fixtures.
- **Fixtures.** Create, edit and delete fixtures between two different
  registered teams with a phase, optional round (1–99), schedule, status and
  score. Fixtures saved without a round are listed under **No round**. League
  fixtures need both teams in the fixture's division; playoff and relegation
  fixtures may cross divisions. Final and forfeit results need both
  whole-number scores; a scheduled fixture never keeps a score.
- **Linking a match.** **Link to a match** opens a side panel to connect the
  fixture to one native Logi match event of the same game. It offers the
  matches of the workspaces linked to either team, both teams assigned and
  nearest the fixture's date first, searchable by name; you can also enter an
  event ID. When the
  event has assigned match teams, both fixture teams must be among them. An
  event can belong to only one fixture. An imported Hell Let Loose result is
  Axis versus Allies, so Logi fills the fixture only when both fixture teams
  have an Axis or Allies side assigned in the event: each team gets its side's
  score. If the event already has such a result, a still-scheduled fixture takes
  it; later imports mark the fixture final. Without sides, enter the score
  yourself. Changing a fixture's teams releases its linked match. Workspace
  managers no longer link their own matches.
- **Deleting.** Deleting a division or a fixture and removing a registered
  team first ask for confirmation and say what changes; none of them can be
  undone.
- **ECL 2026.** The ECL 2026 import is an operator task, not a dashboard
  button: a global administrator's `POST /api/competitions/ecl/seed` creates
  (or completes) the published ECL competition with its official divisions,
  reusing catalogue teams with the same name and creating the missing ones.

Standings use the ECL cap-score rules and count only final and forfeit fixtures
with both scores. Changes reach the public pages within a few minutes. A Logi
result is not a replacement for checking the official competition result,
opponent, division or forfeit rules.

<Callout type="warning">
    Competition records created before global teams referenced Logi workspaces,
    including placeholder clans. Until the platform owner runs the one-time
    migration they stay readable but show **Migration pending**, cannot be
    linked, and block the ECL seed. The migration turns each referenced
    workspace into (or matches it with) a catalogue team of the competition's
    game, linked to the workspace when it is a real Logi workspace. Two
    same-name clans of one competition become one team only when they share a
    division and never played each other; otherwise the second keeps its own
    team with a distinguishable name you can rename. Public pages may show the
    previous state for up to five minutes after the migration.
</Callout>

## LogiComms

Access to elevated pages is separate from ordinary workspace manager access.
If an elevated page or action is absent, do not assume a normal Discord role is
enough; verify the account's platform-level authorisation. Conversely, elevated
access does not bypass Discord's own channel, thread, or role hierarchy checks.

LogiComms is available from the dashboard navigation and is also introduced on the public Logi home page. Use it according to your community’s communication process, and keep operational messaging clear about audience, timing, and action required.

LogiComms is a Windows download rather than a dashboard messaging control. The
dashboard and public home page check the current release manifest when they
load and show a download button only after they receive a valid HTTPS Windows
x86_64 URL. If the button is unavailable, Logi has not established that a safe
current download is available; wait and retry rather than substituting an
unverified link.

<Callout type="warning">
    Elevated access is not a reason to bypass normal checks. Before a
    destructive or broad-impact action, confirm the workspace, the intended
    target, and the user-facing consequences.
</Callout>
